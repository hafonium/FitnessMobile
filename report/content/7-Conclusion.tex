\section{Conclusion}

HomeWorkout demonstrates a complete direction for a local-first fitness application. It connects profile-based plan selection, a substantial exercise catalog, editable plans, a guided player, session persistence, progress features, settings, reminders, voice support, weight and history reporting, and an AI assistant through one Android codebase.

The application is strongest where features share the same data and domain rules. The same exercise records support recommendation, browsing, editing, details, and workout playback. The same completed-session records support goals, streaks, achievements, and history. Clean layer boundaries make these relationships understandable and provide a practical base for further development.

The project has not reached release quality. The chatbot and main development lines must be integrated through a new Room migration, lint must pass, test coverage must be expanded, and the AI key must be moved out of the APK. These are important issues, but they are bounded engineering tasks rather than missing product foundations. After they are addressed, the repository can support a stable demonstration and a credible next iteration focused on context-aware fitness guidance.
